About Aaryan Car Rentals

OUR STORY

Setting the Standard in Gujarat

Founded over a decade ago in Vadodara, Aaryan Car Rentals began with a simple vision: to elevate the standard of ground transportation in Gujarat from merely functional to genuinely exceptional.

We recognized that corporate executives, international delegates, and families celebrating milestones required more than just a vehicle. They needed reliability, strict punctuality, absolute discretion, and the peace of mind that comes from knowing every detail has been handled by professionals.

Today, we operate a meticulously maintained fleet of over 50 premium vehicles, ranging from executive sedans to luxury vans, serving clients across Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat, and beyond. We are proud to be the trusted mobility partner for leading corporate houses, premium hotels, and discerning private clients.

Our Mission

To provide impeccable, safe, and luxurious mobility solutions that exceed client expectations through professional chauffeurs, meticulously maintained vehicles, and a relentless focus on customer service.

Our Vision

To be universally recognized as Gujarat’s most trusted and prestigious executive transport brand, expanding our footprint while maintaining the bespoke, high-touch service of a boutique firm.
